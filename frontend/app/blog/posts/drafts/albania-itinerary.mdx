Albania is one of Europe's last true hidden gems. While the rest of the Mediterranean has become increasingly crowded and expensive, Albania offers stunning mountain ranges, crystal-clear beaches, and a rich history, all at a fraction of the cost of its neighbors.

This 9-day itinerary takes you through the very best of the country, splitting time between the rugged Albanian Alps in the North and the sun-drenched Albanian Riviera in the South.

## Safety in Albania

Albania is generally a very safe country for travelers. The locals are incredibly hospitable and welcoming. As with any destination, use common sense: keep an eye on your belongings in crowded areas and be cautious when driving.

## Rental Car (Alamo)

To truly see Albania, you'll want your own wheels. We rented our car through **Alamo** and had a great experience. Having a rental car allowed us to navigate the mountain passes of the North and the coastal roads of the South at our own pace. 

**Tip:** If you're heading into the North (Theth), ensure your rental agreement allows for mountain driving, and consider a car with decent clearance, though most main roads are now well-paved.

## North vs South: Can you do both?

One of the most common questions when planning an Albania trip is whether to head North to the mountains or South to the beaches. If you have 9 days, the answer is: **both**. 

The North offers dramatic scenery in the Albanian Alps and the serene beauty of Komani Lake. The South is home to the stunning Ionian coast and historic towns like Berat and Gjirokaster. This itinerary is designed to give you a taste of both worlds.

## The Itinerary

### Day 1: Tirane to Komani Lake (North)

After arriving in Tirane, pick up your rental car and head North. The drive to Komani Lake takes about 2.5 hours.

- **Stay:** [Borealis Guest House](https://www.booking.com) – A beautiful spot right on the water.
- **Dinner:** **Bar restaurant "Te Kastrioti"** – Highly recommended for an amazing dinner with local flavors.
- **Drinks:** **Lake Side Lounge** – Relax on bean bag chairs by the lake with a drink in hand.

### Day 2: Komani Lake Day Trip

Spend the day exploring the stunning Komani Lake. The ferry ride through the narrow canyons is often compared to the Norwegian fjords and is a highlight of any trip to Albania. You can take a boat tour that visits remote villages or just enjoy the spectacular scenery from the water.

### Day 3: Theth

Drive from Komani Lake to Theth (approx. 3 hours). The drive takes you through a beautiful mountain pass with incredible views of the Prokletije (Accursed Mountains).

- **Stay:** [Guesthouse Prrockaj](https://www.booking.com) – A traditional and welcoming stay in the heart of the village.

### Day 4: Hiking & Blue Eye

Theth is a hiker's paradise. Spend the day exploring the valley. A must-visit is the **Blue Eye (Syri i Kaltër)** of Theth, a stunning natural spring with vibrant blue water. It's a moderate hike from the village and well worth the effort.

- **Stay:** Guesthouse Prrockaj

### Day 5: Return to Tirane

Head back to the capital, Tirane (approx. 3.5 hour drive). Use the afternoon to dive into Albania's complex history.

- **House of Leaves:** A museum dedicated to the secret surveillance of the communist era.
- **Bunk'Art 1 & 2:** Massive bunkers converted into art and history museums.
- **Cable Car:** Take the Dajti Ekspres cable car for panoramic views of the city.

### Day 6: Saranda (South Coast)

Time to head South! The drive from Tirane to Saranda takes about 4 hours. You'll notice the landscape change from mountains to the sparkling Ionian Sea.

### Day 7: Saranda (Ksamil & Butrint)

Explore the area around Saranda:

- **Ksamil Beach:** Known for its turquoise water and small islands you can swim or boat to.
- **Butrint National Archaeological Park:** A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring ruins from Greek, Roman, Byzantine, and Venetian periods.

### Day 8: Berat

Drive to Berat (approx. 3 hours). On the way, make sure to stop at **Gjirokaster Castle**, which overlooks the "City of Stone."

Berat is known as the "City of a Thousand Windows" due to its well-preserved Ottoman architecture.
- **Optional:** If you have more time, consider an extended stay for a tour to the **Osumi Canyon**.

### Day 9: Return to Tirane

Complete the loop with a 2-hour drive back to Tirane for your flight home.

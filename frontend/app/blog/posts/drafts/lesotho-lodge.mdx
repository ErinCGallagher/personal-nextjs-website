We hadn't heard much about Lesotho before our trip, but it turned out to be one of the most memorable parts of our South African road trip. The country is a hidden gem, offering stunning landscapes, rich culture, and a unique experience for travelers. We drove from Johannesburg to Lesotho in our own rental car, but we also came across a tour group who were doing a similar trip, so there are options for those who don't want to drive themselves.

One of the highlights of our visit was the town of Semonkong and Semonkong Lodge, located in the heart of the Maluti Mountains within the town of Semonkong. The lodge offers breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains and valleys, and it's the perfect base for exploring the natural beauty of Lesotho. We spent three nights at the lodge, participated in various activities, and it was an unforgettable experience.

## Location and Access

Lesotho is a small landlocked country of around 2.4 million people, entirely surrounded by South Africa. The country is known for its mountainous terrain, with it's borders being mostly defined by the Drakensberg and Maloti mountain ranges. This natural fortification has helped Lesotho maintain its independence and unique culture throughout history.

There are two main border crossings between South Africa and Lesotho: the Maseru Bridge Border Post and the Caledonspoort Border Post. The Maseru Bridge Border Post is the most commonly used crossing, as it is located near the capital city of Maseru and is well-connected to major South African cities. We wouldn't recommend the Caledonspoort Border Post because the roads are significantly slower and there is less infrastructure available. There is also access from the Sani Pass border post, which is located in the Drakensberg mountains and is popular among adventure travelers. The Sani Pass is a challenging route that requires a 4x4 vehicle, but it offers stunning views of the surrounding mountains and valleys.

## Safety

Lesotho is considered a very safe place to travel, especially compared to some of its neighboring countries. The crime rate is relatively low, and the people are known for being friendly and welcoming to tourists. However, as with any travel destination, it's important to take common-sense precautions to ensure your safety.

## Accomodation

[Semonkong Lodge Accommodation Details](https://www.semonkonglodge.com/semonkong-lodge-accommodation-and-activity-rates/)

Semonkong Lodge is located in the town of Semonkong, which is about a 3-hour drive from the Maseru Bridge Border Post, or a 8 hour drive from Johanesburg. Once you cross into Lesotho and leave the capital of Maseru, the drive through the mountains is peaceful and beautiful. The exceptionally well maintain road winds through the mountains going up to 2,800 meters, giving you breathtaking scenery along the way. The lodge is situated in a remote area town of Semongkong and we rented a car for the trip, which gave us the flexibility to explore the area at our own pace. However, there are also tour groups that offer transportation to and from the lodge if you prefer not to drive.

They offer three types of accomodation: en-suite rooms, a campsite, and self-catering huts.

### En-Suite Rooms

This is what we chose to stay in and it was so lovely. The rooms are spacious and comfortable, with large windows that offer stunning views of the surrounding mountains. The en-suite bathrooms are clean and well-maintained, providing a convenient and comfortable stay.

### Campsite

The campsite is a great option for those who want to experience the outdoors and save some money. The campsite is on the banks of the Maletsunyane River and accomodates Tents and campervans. They offer braai facilities for cooking and a shared shower facility with hot water.

### Self-Catering Huts

These dormatories are more afforable options that are still very comfortable. They have a shared bathroom and a small kitchenette, which is perfect for those who want to cook their own meals. They offer bunk beds that sleep 6-8 people.

## Food

The lodge has a restaurant called the duck and donkey tavern which offers excellent food and drinks options. We had all our meals here and the food was delicious and reasonably priced. The menu features a variety of dishes, including traditional cuisine and international options. The restaurant also has a great selection of local beers and wines, which are perfect for enjoying while taking in the stunning views. The highlight was the home made bread they made with each meal, it was the best bread we had ever tasted.

## Maletsunyane Falls

This is the main reason most travelers come to Semonkong, and it's easy to see why. The Maletsunyane Falls is the highest single-drop waterfalls in Africa, plunging 192 meters into a deep gorge below. The falls are surrounded by stunning mountain scenery, making it a truly breathtaking sight. The falls are only a 1 hour hike from Semonkong Lodge, and the trail is well-marked and relatively easy to follow. Although optional, we hired a local guide to take us to the falls, which was a great decision. Our guide was knowledgeable about the area and provided interesting insights into the local culture and history. We had a really great time learning about his culture and hearing his stories about the area. The village uses mainly horses and donkeys for transportation, so he was very curious to hear about how a subway worked and how we got around in our daily lives in comparison.

You have a few different options for how to experience the falls. You can hike down to the base of the falls, which is a challenging but rewarding hike that takes about 2-3 hours round trip. The trail is steep and rocky in some sections, so it's important to wear sturdy shoes and be prepared for a bit of a workout. Alternatively, you can take a more leisurely hike to one of the viewpoints along the trail, which is what we did, that offer equally stunning views of the falls without the strenuous hike.

For the more adventurous you can abseil down the falls. The abseil is about 192 meters and offers an incredible adrenaline rush as you descend alongside the cascading water. Semonkong Lodge offers abseiling packages that include all the necessary equipment and a trained guide to ensure a safe and unforgettable experience. It's an incredible way to experience the falls up close and is definitely something to consider if you're looking for an adventure.

## Other Activities

[Semonkong Lodge Activites](https://www.semonkonglodge.com/activities-what-to-do-in-lesotho/)

Semonkong Lodge offers a variety of other activities that allow you to explore the natural beauty of Lesotho. You can go horseback riding through the mountains, which is a great way to take in the stunning scenery and experience the local culture. The lodge also offers guided hikes and mountain biking to nearby attractions. Additionally, you can go fishing in the nearby rivers or simply relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings of the lodge. They also offer 4x4 tours to explore the surrounding area, which is a great way to see more of the stunning landscapes and learn about the local culture. The tours take you to nearby villages, where you can interact with the locals and learn about their way of life.

Another fun experience they offer is the Donkey pub crawl, where you can visit local bars and pubs on the back of a donkey. It's a unique and fun way to experience the local nightlife and interact with the friendly locals. They also offer the opportunity to participate in a traditional blanket ceremony. The ceremony involves being wrapped in a traditional Basotho blanket and learning about the significance of the blanket in Basotho culture. It's a great way to learn about the local culture and traditions while also having a fun and memorable experience and a souvenir at the end.
